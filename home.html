About Children's Rights

Understanding and protecting every child's future.

What Are Children's Rights?

Children's rights are the basic freedoms and protections that every child deserves regardless of their background, gender, religion, disability or social status.

These rights help children grow, learn, stay healthy and live free from violence, discrimination and neglect.

Our Mission

To create awareness, promote equality and protect every child's rights for a brighter future.

Every Child Has Rights

Here are six important rights every child should enjoy.

📚

Right to Education

Every child deserves access to quality education and opportunities to learn.

🏥

Right to Healthcare

Every child has the right to medical care, good health and proper nutrition.

🏠

Right to Shelter

Every child deserves a safe home, clothing and basic necessities.

🛡️

Right to Protection

Children must be protected against abuse, exploitation, violence and neglect.

⚖️

Right to Equality

All children deserve equal treatment without discrimination.

🗣️

Right to Be Heard

Every child has the right to express their views and opinions.

Protect Every Child

Children deserve a world free from fear and violence.

Say NO to Child Abuse

Child abuse includes physical, emotional and sexual abuse, neglect and exploitation. Every child deserves safety, respect and dignity.

How Can We Protect Children?

  • Report suspected child abuse.
  • Provide children with safe environments.
  • Encourage children to speak freely.
  • Promote access to education.
  • Teach children about their rights.

Children's Rights Laws

Legal frameworks that protect children.

United Nations Convention on the Rights of the Child

The UN Convention on the Rights of the Child establishes international standards for protecting children's civil, political, economic, social and cultural rights.

Children Act, 2022 – Kenya

Kenya's Children Act, 2022 provides a legal framework for children's care, protection, welfare and rights.
